Set up your panel

Two ways to get a Game Day Scoreboard on Wi-Fi and following your team.

1. Plug it in

Connect the M-1 to power over USB.

2. Get the app

The Game Day app finds the panel on your network and walks through Wi-Fi setup over Bluetooth. It's coming soon to the App Store.

Once it's installed, open the app and follow Set up a new panel.

No app yet? Use the browser instead

The installer page flashes and sets up the panel from a desktop browser over USB, no app required. Click Install, then enter your Wi-Fi details in the dialog that follows.

If you skip that step, or you're adding a second panel, it starts a hotspot called Game Day Scoreboard. Join it from any phone or laptop and a setup page opens.

3. Pick your team

Once it's on Wi-Fi, the panel shows where to go: open the gameday-xxxxxx.local address it displays, or the numeric address underneath if your phone can't resolve .local names. Pick your team on that page and it starts following the game.
