Game Day Scoreboard

Football scoreboard for the Apollo M-1 HUB75 matrix. Version __VERSION__.

1. Install

Plug the M-1 into this computer over USB. Use Chrome or Edge; Firefox and Safari cannot talk to serial ports. One firmware covers a single 64x64 panel and two panels side by side; you pick which on the device page afterwards.

This browser cannot install firmware. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Open this page over HTTPS to install.

Installing erases whatever firmware was on the M-1. Your WiFi details and team choice are asked for afterwards.

2. Join WiFi

When the install finishes the same dialog offers to connect the device to WiFi. Enter your network name and password. If you skip it, the panel starts a hotspot called Game Day Scoreboard; join it and a setup page opens.

3. Pick your team

The panel itself tells you where to go once it's online: open the gameday-xxxxxx.local name it shows on your phone, or the address shown under it if your phone doesn't resolve .local names.

  1. Choose your team in the Team dropdown (NFL and college).
  2. Two panels side by side? Click the two-panel picture under Panels. The matrix restarts using both.
  3. Set your Timezone so kickoff times read right.
  4. Optional: turn ticker parts and opponent splashes on or off.

The panel shows the next game, switches to the live layout at kickoff, celebrates scores, and lingers on the final. Everything is fetched from ESPN by the device itself. No Home Assistant needed, though the device does show up there if you want it.

Source, issues and the YAML for building it yourself: bharvey88/gameday-scoreboard. Built on hub75-studio and ESPHome.
