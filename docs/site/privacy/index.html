Privacy

Effective 2026-10-06.

The short version

There's no account, no cloud service, and nothing tracking you. The app and the panel talk to each other over your home network, and the panel talks to ESPN for scores and to GitHub for firmware updates. That's the whole picture.

What the panel does

The panel asks ESPN's public site for your team's schedule and scores, and downloads team logos from ESPN's image servers. It checks this site for firmware updates every six hours, and its own web page can look up release notes from GitHub. None of that carries any information about you, only the team you picked and a request for the current schedule.

What the app does

The app finds and talks to panels on your home Wi-Fi network only, using Bonjour to discover them and HTTP to read scores and send settings. First-time Wi-Fi setup happens over Bluetooth, directly between your phone and the panel. Your Wi-Fi password goes from the phone straight to the panel and is stored only on the panel. The app also fetches team logos from ESPN's image servers so you can see them while picking a team. The app has no server of its own, no account or sign-in, and does not collect analytics or usage data.

If you buy one or email us

Orders go through our Shopify store at gameday-scoreboard.com, which keeps your name, shipping address, email and order details so we can ship your scoreboard and handle returns and warranty claims. Payments are processed by Shopify; we never see your full card number. Shipping labels go through UPS.

When you email support@gameday-scoreboard.com, we keep your message, name and email address so we can answer you and help with warranty claims later. Support email is handled with Cloudflare (our help desk and an AI tool that drafts replies; a person reads and sends every reply), Google (a backup copy) and Resend (sends our replies). We delete closed support conversations after two years.

We only email you about your order or your support request. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you sign up for one yourself. To have your data deleted, email support@gameday-scoreboard.com and we'll remove it.

What we don't do

No accounts. No analytics or crash reporting in the app or on the panel. No advertising. We don't sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the services listed above that we need to run the store and support.

Like any website, ESPN and GitHub (which hosts this site and the firmware updates) see the IP address a request comes from. We don't receive or keep any of that.

Questions

Email support@gameday-scoreboard.com or use the support page.
